# ShudderCam Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 23 August 2026**

ShudderCam is a camera app made by Huestone Holdings. This policy describes
exactly what the app does with your information. It is short because the app
does very little.

## The short version

ShudderCam collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to. It has no network
permission at all.

## The camera

The camera feed is processed entirely on your device, in memory, to draw the
viewfinder. Nothing about that feed leaves your phone, and nothing from it is
written to storage unless you press the shutter.

## Photos you take

When you press the shutter, the photo is saved to your device's own media
library, in Pictures/ShudderCam. From that moment it is an ordinary photo on
your phone: ShudderCam does not copy it, upload it, index it, or read it back
apart from drawing the small thumbnail in the corner of the viewfinder. Tapping
that thumbnail hands the photo to whichever gallery app you already use.

Deleting those photos is done the same way you delete any other photo. Removing
ShudderCam does not remove pictures you took with it.

## Settings

Your camera settings — control overrides, saved presets, TrueSight calibration,
and mode preferences — are stored on your device so the app works the way you
left it. They stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

## What ShudderCam does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in, no profile.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
- No network access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it
  cannot transmit anything even in principle.
- No location collection. Photos are not tagged with your location by the app.
- No sharing of any information with third parties, because none is gathered.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera** — required, and the whole point: it draws the viewfinder and takes
  the photo.
- **Storage (Android 9 and older only)** — needed to write your photos into
  Pictures/ShudderCam on those versions. Android 10 and newer do not need it.

The app also declares one app-private permission,
`com.huestone.shuddercam.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`, which
Android's support libraries add automatically so that the component watching
your battery level cannot be reached by other apps. It grants ShudderCam no
access to anything.

## Children

ShudderCam is a general-audience camera app and gathers no personal information
from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes, this policy will be updated before the
change ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
